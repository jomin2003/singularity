Privacy Policy — SINGULARITY

Last updated: 13 September 2026 · Applies to version 1.0

The short version: SINGULARITY collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no trackers and no servers. Everything the game remembers stays on your phone. Optional rewarded ads are provided by Google AdMob.

Information we collect

None. The game does not collect, transmit, store or share any personal or sensitive information. We do not receive any data from your device.

Data stored on your device

The game keeps a small amount of data in its own local storage on your device, so it remembers you between sessions:

  • Your best score and the last five run scores with their dates, shown on the menu.
  • Your preferences — sound and music/effects volumes, haptics strength, motion, colour-vision mode, text size, high contrast, threat readout, first-encounter pause and control scheme.
  • A flag recording whether the first-run hints have already been shown.

This data never leaves your device, is not backed up to us, and is deleted automatically when you uninstall the game or clear the app's storage.

Network access

SINGULARITY is fully playable offline. The core game makes no network requests of any kind — no downloads, no telemetry, no crash reporting. Network access is only used to load optional rewarded ads if you choose to view them. All graphics are generated by the app itself at runtime, and the ambient soundtrack plays from audio files bundled inside the app.

Third parties

The app contains the Google AdMob SDK to provide optional rewarded ads. We do not use any analytics SDKs, social integrations or other third-party code. We do not sell or share any information ourselves. AdMob processes data to provide ads according to its own policies.

Permissions

The app requests the INTERNET permission to load optional ads, and the VIBRATE permission for haptic feedback. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or any other sensitive data.

Children

SINGULARITY is not directed at children under 13 and contains no age-restricted content. Because no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children either.

Your rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct, export or delete. Uninstalling the app removes its local save data completely.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact listed on the app's Google Play store listing.
